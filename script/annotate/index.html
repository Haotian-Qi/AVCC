Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Play / Pause Space
  • Word selected: edit its text (Enter again applies it). Nothing selected: select the word under the playhead on the current lane Enter
  • Deselect / leave a text field Escape
  • Switch speaker lane; a selected word or group moves to it ↑ / ↓
  • Move the selected word by 0.01 s ← / →
  • Snap start to the previous word's end / end to the next word's start Shift+← / Shift+→
  • Word selected: extend the left / right edge by 0.01 s (Caps Lock on: shrink) , / .
  • Wheel over the selected word: up = left edge, down = right edge; extend, or shrink with Caps Lock on (0.01 s, Shift 0.05 s) Wheel
  • Drag a word: the end handles drag that edge; Caps Lock on: grab it anywhere to move it Drag
  • Select a group: drag a window over empty lane space (Shift/Ctrl adds, Ctrl+click toggles one word); ↑/↓ or the yellow bar sets their speaker Drag
  • Nothing selected: previous / next frame (Shift: 5 frames) , / .
  • Next / previous word on the current lane Tab / Shift+Tab
  • Add a word at the playhead + / =
  • Split at the playhead: the selected word, else the one on the current lane; selects the right piece (Auto-split on: the left piece keeps the first word, the right piece the rest) B
  • Delete the selected word Delete
  • Undo the last delete, split or group speaker change (up to 10) Ctrl+Z
  • Scroll the timeline back to the playhead P
  • Playback speed, 0.5x to 3.0x (works while typing) PgUp / PgDn
  • Hide / show the video list \
  • Bbox mode: edit the face boxes (its keys are listed on its bar) E
